Bio

A producer/DJ hailing from Nottingham and based in London, into an eclectic mix ranging through techno, jungle, breaks, UK garage and 140 & bass.

A resident DJ at Mischief and formerly at TUSH, Zerdazi has played at Leeds, Glastonbury and TRNSMT festivals, Burning Man in the Nevada desert and London Decompression. Further afield there have been sets at Ibiza.PlayAbout, the Lollapalooza bar in Osaka and a TUSH takeover at COCO in Lille, along with bills shared with Mak & Pasteman and Reimond and a radio show on Mode London.
